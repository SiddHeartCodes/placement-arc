Before anything else

"This one thing cracks 95% of placements"

A senior said that in a seminar, then immediately walked the number back. It isn't literal — and it isn't the point.

What people assume
  • DSA is #1, everything else is optional
  • 500 problems solved guarantees an offer
  • One impressive tech stack wins over every interviewer
  • Every company runs the same set of rounds
What's actually true
  • Every company has its own hiring mindset and structure
  • Different rounds test different domains, on different days
  • Strength across many domains raises your odds everywhere
  • Nothing in this list outranks anything else

Everything on your resume is a question you volunteered for

If you can't defend a line in under sixty seconds — the tool, the project, the number — it doesn't belong on the page. Unsupported breadth reads worse than honest depth.

Preparation isn't a guarantee

preparation → higher probability  ·  luck → still random

Interviewer, exact question, headcount, timing, who else is in the room — some of it you will never control. The stack doesn't remove randomness. It stacks the odds in your favor, round after round.
